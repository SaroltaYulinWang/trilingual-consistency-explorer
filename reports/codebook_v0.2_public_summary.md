# When Translations Disagree — Codebook v0.2 Summary

**Approved Codebook v0.2 summary. The five-category framework, D1–D5 decision sequence, annotation protocol and justified typo-recovery policy were approved by the sole researcher on 22 November 2026.**

This project audits semantic correspondence in English–Chinese–Hungarian triples from Tatoeba. English is a reference pivot, not necessarily the historical original. Community translation links are evidence of linkage, not proof of equivalence. EN–ZH and EN–HU are assessed separately before assigning one triple-level category.

| Primary category | Operational meaning | 中文说明 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Core Meaning Preserved | Normal differences in grammar, wording, or common expressions between Chinese, English, and Hungarian do not count as a meaning change. As long as both translations carry the same main message and purpose, these natural differences are acceptable. | 中文、英語和匈牙利語之間正常的語法、措辭或慣用表達差異，不算作意義變化。只要兩個譯文傳達相同的主要訊息和目的，這些自然差異便可接受。 |
| Acceptable Variation | A noticeable, non-routine change in word choice, tone, or viewpoint can be acceptable if the main message and communicative purpose are preserved. The change just needs to make good sense for the context or fit how people naturally speak in that language. | 只要主要訊息和交際目的得以保留，詞語選擇、語氣或觀點上明顯而非例行的變化也可以接受。這種變化只需在語境中合理，或符合該語言自然的表達方式。 |
| Divergent Interpretations | Sometimes the English pivot sentence has more than one valid meaning. When this happens, the Chinese and Hungarian translations might pick different meanings, and both choices are reasonable. | 英語樞軸句有時具有多於一種合理意義。在這種情況下，中文與匈牙利語譯文可能各自選取不同的意義，而兩種選擇都合理。 |
| Potential Accuracy Issue | You might suspect an issue when a specific part of a translation seems to add, drop, or change a meaning supported by the English pivot without a good reason. However, this is just a warning sign to double-check, not proof of a definite mistake. | 當譯文的某個具體部分似乎在沒有充分理由的情況下，增添、遺漏或改變了英語樞軸所支持的意義，便可能有問題。然而，這只是提醒進一步核查的警號，並非確定有錯的證據。 |
| Indeterminate | You cannot make a reliable classification when important context or other evidence needed to interpret the sentences is missing. This label simply means there is not enough information to judge, not that the translations are wrong. | 如果理解句子所需的重要語境或其他證據缺失，便無法作出可靠分類。此標籤只表示資料不足以判斷，並不表示譯文有錯。 |

The decision order is: specific content concern; material unresolved question; actual reading split; substantive acceptable variation; otherwise supported core correspondence. Source ambiguity alone does not establish divergence. Different morphology or ordinary idioms do not establish substantive variation. Orthographic observations, including suspected_typo, are auxiliary; a misspelling does not automatically establish a semantic error. Any recovery-dependent assessment requires the approved recovery policy and appropriate human judgment.

These five categories are a **custom research scheme**, informed by [MQM Accuracy (MQM Council, n.d.)](https://www.themqm.org/mqm-pillars/typology/), not official MQM categories or scores. Additional foundations are [Freitag et al. (2021)](https://aclanthology.org/2021.tacl-1.87/) on explicit evaluation and context, and [Francesch and Payrató (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1111/stul.12219) on ambiguity and interpretation.

Original pilot judgments, AI suggestions and later researcher decisions remain distinct. AI-only annotation is never marked human-checked automatically. Neither the 30-record pilot nor these definitions establishes an error rate or independent annotation agreement. Detailed authentic examples and the final approval entry are in the [full Codebook](codebook_v0.2_publication.md). Its earlier PENDING/CONDITIONAL entries are preserved development history, superseded by the dated approval record.
